06 — AI / ML DEEP DIVE

The four engines, under the hood

🧠

E1 · SAR–Optical Change Segmentation

Arch: Siamese Swin-Transformer encoder + UNet decoder (ChangeFormer / BIT baseline), shared weights, difference-attention fusion module.

Input (6-ch, 256×256): σ⁰VV, σ⁰VH, Δσ⁰log-ratio, InSAR coherence, NDVI, BSI — bi-temporal stack.

Training: SimCLR/MoCo self-supervised pre-training on ~1 M unlabelled SAR patches → fine-tune on ~500 labelled mine polygons. Dice + Focal loss (class imbalance <1%).

Output: per-pixel change probability → CRF/morphological cleanup → vectorised polygon + confidence.

TARGET F1-SCORE0.90
📦

E2 · Volumetric Quantification

Method: DEM-of-Difference — DEMt1 − DEMt0 over the change mask; V = Σ(ΔZ · cell area).

Sources: CartoDEM / Cartosat-3 stereo photogrammetry, TanDEM-X, InSAR-derived DEM, and a monocular depth CNN refined on shadow-length cues for high-res optical.

Rigour: co-registration bias removal + Level-of-Detection (LoD₉₅) uncertainty propagation → volume reported with ± error bar (only statistically significant change is flagged).

Economics: V × bulk density × state royalty rate → ₹ revenue loss, cross-checked with e-transit-pass / weighbridge declarations.

VOLUME ACCURACY±10–15%
📉

E3 · Unsafe-Mining & Deformation

PS-InSAR / SBAS (ISCE2 + MintPy) over Sentinel-1 SLC stacks → line-of-sight velocity maps at mm/year: highwall creep, pit-wall instability, underground-mine subsidence.

Geometry audit: DEM → slope angle, bench height, bench width, overhang & approach-road gradient, checked against DGMS / MMR-1961 & CMR-2017 thresholds.

Add-ons: Landsat/VIIRS thermal anomaly for coal-seam fires (Jharia-type); NDWI for flooded/abandoned pits; distance-to-habitation risk.

Output: a per-lease “Mine Health Score” (0–100) + prioritised inspection queue for DGMS.

🕰️

E4 · Temporal Reasoning (false-alarm killer)

Why: ploughed fields, brick kilns, construction, flooding and monsoon soil moisture all mimic a fresh pit in a single SAR pair. This is the #1 cause of failure in real deployments.

Model: Temporal Transformer / LSTM over a 36-month σ⁰ series per pixel + BFAST/CUSUM breakpoint detection → distinguishes cyclical from monotonic, irreversible change.

Context classifier: LULC head separates mining from construction/agriculture; brick-kiln & water-body masks applied.

Human-in-the-loop: field verdicts stream back → active learning retraining every quarter.

FALSE-POSITIVE REDUCTION TARGET−60%
